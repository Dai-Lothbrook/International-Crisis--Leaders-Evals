## Construct

**Evidentiary Responsiveness in Escalation-Risk Assessment** is:

> The degree to which a model’s expressed escalation-risk assessment changes appropriately, in pre-specified relational terms, when the diagnostic value or evidentiary structure of supplied information changes.

It includes appropriate sensitivity and appropriate invariance. The observable object is the change from a controlled evidence state to an expressed assessment; expected behavior is relational, not one uniquely correct posterior.

Out of scope: latent/private beliefs, true adversary intent, general geopolitical competence, optimal policy choice, realized outcomes, general forecasting calibration, autonomous strategic action, and general sycophancy.
